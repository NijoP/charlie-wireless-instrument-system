# Decision and Assumption Register

This register documents key decisions made and assumptions assumed during the engineering documentation phase (Phase 0) of the Charlie Wireless Instrument System project. These decisions and assumptions are based on the RFQ and engineering judgment, and should be reviewed and validated in subsequent phases.

## Assumptions Made

| Assumption ID | Description | Rationale | Potential Impact if Incorrect | Related Requirements / Risks |
|---------------|-------------|-----------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| ASSM-001 | The TX battery capacity scalability from ~600 mAh to ~1000 mAh assumes similar form factor and chemistry. | Based on RFQ statement "Scalable to ~1000 mAh." | If scaling requires larger footprint, TX mechanical design may need revision. | REQ-010, RISK-003 |
| ASSM-002 | The RX unit will also use a 3.7 V LiPo battery for portable operation (if battery-powered), though capacity is not specified in RFQ. | Inferred from TX battery and portable nature of RX. | If RX battery chemistry or voltage differs, power design may need adjustment. | REQ-004 (implied), RISK-004 |
| ASSM-003 | The audio signal path is mono; stereo is not required. | RFQ specifies mono in audio minimum. | If stereo is later needed, audio chain would require duplication. | REQ-013 |
| ASSM-004 | The TX instrument pickup input is a high-impedance analog signal suitable for direct connection to an analog front-end (no phantom power or preamp needed). | Typical for instrument pickups (e.g., electric guitar). | If pickup requires biasing or preamp, analog front-end design may need adjustment. | REQ-018 |
| ASSM-005 | The digital MEMS microphone on TX outputs a standard digital audio format (e.g., I2S or PDM) compatible with the processor. | Common for MEMS microphones. | If microphone uses a proprietary interface, additional conversion circuitry may be needed. | REQ-019 |
| ASSM-006 | The TX metadata generation does not include complete FFT bins unless technically justified; metadata size is assumed to be small enough not to dominate wireless bandwidth. | Based on RFQ statement. | If metadata requires FFT bins, wireless bandwidth may be insufficient. | REQ-025, RISK-002 |
| ASSM-007 | The wireless link latency budget can be allocated such that wireless transmission and reception latency is a small fraction of the total end-to-end budget (<5 ms or 10 ms). | Based on RFQ focus on designing RF subsystem for lowest practical packetization and buffering latency. | If wireless latency is inherently high (e.g., >10 ms), the latency target may be unattainable. | REQ-029, RISK-001 |
| ASSM-008 | The STM32H7 selected will have sufficient processing power (MIPS) and memory to run the required DSP algorithms in real-time. | Based on the RFQ listing extensive DSP capabilities; assumes a capable MCU is available. | If STM32H7 is insufficient, a more powerful MCU or DSP offload may be needed. | REQ-035, RISK-006 |
| ASSM-009 | The optional ESP32-S3 on RX Layer 2, if present, will be used for non-critical tasks and will not interfere with the real-time audio processing on STM32H7. | Based on RFQ requirement that ESP32-S3 should not sit in the critical audio path. | If ESP32-S3 causes interference or latency, audio quality may suffer. | REQ-038, RISK-014 |
| ASSM-010 | The audio codec selected will support 24-bit resolution and 48 kHz minimum sample rate, with low-noise ADC and high-quality DAC. | Based on RFQ audio quality requirements. | If no such codec is available or affordable, audio quality may be compromised. | REQ-043, REQ-044 |
| ASSM-011 | The Class-D amplifier selected will be capable of delivering ~20 W into an 8 Ω load with reasonable efficiency (>85%). | Based on RFQ target. | If amplifier cannot meet power or efficiency targets, thermal and battery life may be affected. | REQ-046 |
| ASSM-012 | The USB-C PD controller will support negotiation of voltages up to 20V and currents sufficient to power the system and charge the battery simultaneously. | Based on RFQ USB-C and PD requirements. | If PD controller lacks sufficient power, system may not reach full amplifier output. | REQ-031, REQ-058 |
| ASSM-013 | The LiPo charger IC will support charging the battery while the system is operating (simultaneous charging and operation). | Based on RFQ requirement. | If charger does not support simultaneous operation, battery charging may require system suspension. | REQ-057 |
| ASSM-014 | The power-path management IC will allow seamless transition between USB-C power, battery power, and combined operation without interruption. | Based on RFQ requirement for simultaneous charging and operation. | If power-path causes glitches during transition, system may reset or audio may drop out. | REQ-057 |
| ASSM-015 | The DC/DC converters for system rails and amplifier rail will have adequate efficiency (>90% for rails, >85% for amplifier rail) to minimize thermal load. | Based on typical expectations for modern converters. | If converters are inefficient, thermal management may be challenging. | REQ-058, RISK-008 |
| ASSM-016 | The eMMC selected will be 16 GB with sufficient read/write speeds for firmware storage, logging, and occasional AI model loading. | Based on RFQ target. | If eMMC is too slow, boot time or logging performance may suffer. | REQ-061 |
| ASSM-017 | The display selected (1.91" AMOLED, 240x536) is commercially available and usable with standard interfaces (SPI, I2C). | Based on RFQ specification. | If display is not available or requires complex interface, UI design may need adjustment. | REQ-032 |
| ASSM-018 | The enclosure volume constraint (~115 cm³ usable) is a conservative estimate for component placement and does not include space for connectors, wiring, or mounting features. | Based on RFQ statement. | If actual usable volume is less, component placement may become difficult. | REQ-005, REQ-006, RISK-011 |
| ASSM-019 | The acoustic compensation via DSP can address a significant portion of the driver/enclosure frequency response but cannot overcome fundamental physical limits (excursion, SPL, resonance, thermal, cabinet volume, port limits, driver efficiency). | Based on RFQ statement. | If DSP overcompensation is attempted, instability or artifacts may occur. | REQ-045, RISK-011 |
| ASSM-020 | The system will be firmware-upgradable via USB-C (for TX and RX) to allow fixes and feature additions post-deployment. | Inferred from modern product practices and USB-C presence. | If upgradability is not designed in, field updates may require hardware replacement. | Not explicitly in RFQ, but implied by USB-C and debugging interface. |
| ASSM-021 | The wireless link will use a proprietary protocol designed for low latency and will not rely on standard Bluetooth LE audio profiles (which may have higher latency). | Based on RFQ distinction between prototype/production candidates and mention of proprietary protocol. | If attempting to use standard Bluetooth audio, latency may exceed target. | REQ-027, REQ-039, RISK-001 |
| ASSM-022 | The TX and RX units will be designed to operate within typical indoor temperature ranges (0°C to 40°C) and will not require industrial temperature ratings unless specified. | Based on typical consumer/product use. | If temperature extremes are encountered, component selection may need revisiting. | RISK-008 (thermal) |
| ASSM-023 | The mechanical design will accommodate the specified batteries (~600-1000 mAh LiPo) with appropriate connectors and retention. | Based on RFQ battery specification. | If battery size assumption is wrong, mechanical redesign may be needed. | REQ-009, REQ-010 |
| ASSM-024 | The TX and RX antennas for the wireless link will be designed to achieve the desired range (to be clarified) while fitting within the enclosure constraints. | Based on need for wireless functionality. | If antenna design conflicts with enclosure or component placement, performance may suffer. | RISK-001, RISK-002 |
| ASSM-025 | The firmware development will follow a modular approach, separating concerns (audio processing, wireless, power management, UI, etc.) to facilitate testing and maintenance. | Based on standard software engineering practices. | If firmware becomes monolithic, debugging and updates may be difficult. | Not explicitly in RFQ, but implied by complex system. |

## Notes
- **Decisions** are things we have determined based on the RFQ (e.g., "we will use USB-C charging") or reasonable interpretations thereof.
- **Assumptions** are things we have taken as true for the purpose of documentation and modeling, but that require verification (e.g., "the STM32H7 will have sufficient MIPS").
- All assumptions should be investigated and either validated or revised in the MATLAB model phase and subsequent feasibility studies.
- Decisions that are directly from the RFQ are marked as "Fixed by RFQ" and should not be changed without client consultation.
- This register will be updated in later phases as decisions are finalized and assumptions are validated or corrected.
